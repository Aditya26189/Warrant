## Scientific content

The central evidence is:

- Discrimination and operating-point trade-offs on the recorded benchmark.
- A constructed context-transfer comparison whose rolling-maximum hypothesis
  did not hold.
- Configuration-dependent PII measurements and their challenge-set limits.
- Declared-workload and monitoring simulations.
- The distinction between artifact agreement, point replay and justified
  statistical interpretation.
- Dirty-source provenance and the open adaptive-bound issue.

Closed grader, unscored-record and stopping-feedback studies are described
with their original outcome and scope.

## Zenodo preparation

The repository includes a prepared metadata file and a reproducible source
bundle recipe. Aditya Singh is the declared author, with the affiliation
supplied for this preprint. No ORCID, DOI, email or additional credential is
invented.

The development repository was private during preparation. Public
reproducibility therefore requires depositing the accompanying reviewed
source/evidence package or otherwise making the needed material accessible.
Private untracked review files and ignored extraction caches are excluded.

The appropriate prepared record is a publication preprint, separate from a
software release. Depositing it does not make it peer reviewed. Upload and
publication remain the author's action; this task does not publish to Zenodo.
