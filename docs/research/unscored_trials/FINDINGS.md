`572b2614be2c0cb2527e14f5b1e4026f1072e6c1`. Complete nonrecursive listings identify
75 submissions and 245 jobs. The census requests 33,048 result files and parses
32,803 named trial records. One job-summary URL remains HTTP 404; all trial-result
files were recovered. Thirty records required metadata-qualified larger read
limits. Failed HTTP requests are retrieval history, not missing executions.

The [generated summary](../../../results/unscored_trials/20261029-terminal-bench2/summary.json)

No paid compute/API services were added.

The original Marin/tau baseline was preserved. The archive hash and all 160
manifest entries verified, original-file snapshots stayed identical, and all 15
numerical JSON/CSV outputs reproduced byte-for-byte in a separate copy. Its 21
tests passed. The [baseline receipt](../../../results/unscored_trials/20261029-terminal-bench2/context/baseline_verification.json)
also retains the cap correction: eight retained cells exceed 5% missingness.
The full-panel 5% row is hypothetical. The historical tau control concerns 4,448
scored final slots and supplies no pre-release retry history.

## Outcomes and native reporting

| Raw named-final outcome | Records |
|---|---:|
| Recorded positive score | 20,648 |
| Recorded zero | 11,229 |
| Null verifier, genuinely unscored in this export | 926 |

All scored rewards in this release are binary. Valid scores coexist with
exceptions in 4,472 records, including 845 positive scores. An exception label
therefore cannot universally replace a score with zero. It does not establish a
cause either.

Pinned January and May Harbor source maps absent verifier/rewards to zero for
the supported single-channel final-record mean. Native matching evaluations
include 81 with both unscored records and positive reward mass, so zero handling
is not inferred only from all-zero examples. It is already an operational native
rule; free unknown-outcome completion is another target, not a repaired score.

The strict **joint** reconciliation statuses are 217 matching jobs, 20 unresolved
and eight unavailable. These test identity, indexes and counters as well as
arithmetic. They must not be reported as 20 numerical score errors. The
[native table](../../../results/unscored_trials/20261029-terminal-bench2/native_score_reproduction.csv)
and indexed job checks preserve each dimension.

Post hoc all-job explanation (U007) separates whole-job arithmetic from native
group reconciliation. An assistant source check of the 28 nonmatching jobs finds
19 matching whole-job means, three differing means and six without one supported
published mean. Pooling for this diagnostic does not repair group identities or
change primary eligibility.

| Differing exported account | Raw final-record mean | Summary mean | Supported explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| BashAgent / TermiGen | 86/445 | 72/445 | Reward/index evidence differs across exports; equal counts do not establish equal outcomes. |
| Capy / Opus 4.6 | 68/89 | 21/29 | Summary indexes a smaller named population; both means are internally coherent. |
| grok-4.2 part 1 | 81/130 | 81/131 | An additional summary-indexed name has recorded zero but no corresponding raw file. It is not an unknown reward. |

Other flags include model/source spelling, OCI/local task identity, reward/error
counter representations and renamed trial indexes. MAYA means match despite
index/counter differences. Crux's custom task pass rate is a different target
from its trial mean. These alternatives prevent an arithmetic-bug conclusion.
The exact submitted harness and export/adjudication history remain unavailable.

## Comparisons that the fixed corpus supports

The frozen primary eligibility rules retain 56 submissions. Fifty-four share one
complete 89-task Git/checksum signature; the other two have singleton signatures.
Of 1,540 eligible unordered pairs, 109 are excluded for incompatible task
definitions/checksums. All 1,431 compatible pairs are analyzed:

| Exact equal-task completion classification | Pairs |
|---|---:|
| Strict robust order | 1,360 |
| Reversal-compatible completion | 56 |
| Equality-compatible ambiguity | 14 |
| Identified tie | 1 |

These are independently free [0,1] completions of present unscored outcomes on
the same retained population. They are not observed corrected rankings or
estimates of capability under repaired infrastructure. Matching task IDs does
not tighten the difference interval `[L_A-H_B, H_A-L_B]` without further
restrictions. No missing planned slots were invented.

Equal-task and pooled-final policies give exactly equal primary scores: the
maximum absolute difference is zero. The retained repetitions provide no
weighting effect in this cohort. This is a negative result conditional on the
conservative eligibility rules, not a claim about every released submission.
No UUID aliases/conflicts or shared cross-submission UUIDs were found.

A descriptive ledger supports raw populations for 64 submissions, including
eight outside the native-reconciled primary cohort. It supplies no secondary
ranking and does not resolve their summaries or system mixtures. All exclusions
remain in [eligibility](../../../results/unscored_trials/20261029-terminal-bench2/eligibility.csv)
and [raw support](../../../results/unscored_trials/20261029-terminal-bench2/raw_population_support.csv).

## Verification and limits

The canonical run used clean source `92f162a8fee29f8f65bdd9293269e0bacb4d6411`,
configuration SHA-256 `b00bbb68f957ee89345e1451f6d07243f276ce2c879491f5c993241e4b28f683`.
Its blocked-network replay matched all 107 canonical files byte-for-byte;
time/environment provenance is retained separately. Source-level and synthetic
tests cover outcome distinctions, scored exceptions, source corruption,
identity/version conflicts, exact bounds, cap rejection and unsupported retry
lineage. Actual test receipts and earlier failed checks are retained with the
results. The post hoc explanation has separate source/provenance.

The [human audit worksheet](../../../results/unscored_trials/20261029-terminal-bench2/validation/human_audit_worksheet.csv)
remains PENDING; assistant cross-checks are not independent human adjudication.
Original planned slots, full attempt history and official website import/
adjudication reproduction remain unavailable. HAL was not used as a fallback:
the narrowed Terminal-Bench audit passed feasibility, and a cleaned response
matrix would not repair these missing records.

The strongest objection is closest-work overlap plus unresolved export causes.
See [RELATED_WORK.md](RELATED_WORK.md) and [CLAIM_LEDGER.md](CLAIM_LEDGER.md).
The evidence supports preserving a technical report and stopping this decision
study, with no manuscript submission claim.
