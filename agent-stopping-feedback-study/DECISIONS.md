# Stopping-feedback study decisions

## S001 — Separate bounded feasibility study, 2026-10-30 UTC

The written brief authorizes this new directory and local research branch only.
The unscored-trials REPORT is closed; its code, claims, artifacts and tags remain
untouched. No remote publishing, author contact, paid inference or GPU work.
Use public recorded executor outputs and CPU methods. Start with metadata and

Existing repository product/probe contracts are not extended by this experiment.

## S002 — Gate A: narrow question eligible for data feasibility

Primary reviews show that abort-only economics, executor/benchmark transfer,
issue-difficulty controls, selective-label identification, randomized auditing,
finite-population retention certificates and stratification are already studied.
None of those is a proposed methodological contribution here.

The concrete remaining empirical question is whether a naturally shifted joint
distribution of stop score, final success and remaining execution expense makes
feedback acquisition erase a previously useful frozen stopping policy's benefit.
The distinguishing experiment would compare heterogeneous suffix expense with
a cost-homogenized control, uniform with established stratified continuation at
matched expenditure, and matched/shifted executors with issue-only controls.
If generic sample-size effects or a fixed-budget baseline explain the whole
result, it supplies no substantial new finding. Gate A permits only data
feasibility; it does not establish novelty or publication merit.

The statistical mapping to Mathematics 14(18):3427 is explicit: candidate sets
are proposed abandonments, adverse candidate labels are recorded successful
runs, and audited successes are restored by original continuation. Its released
adverse fraction maps to lost-success share. We claim no new estimator from
this relabeling. Current-window audit uncertainty is separate from future-workload
generalization; no retrospective check protects the same window in advance.

## S003 — Freeze limited schema/access sample before policy experiments

Inspect at most four initial records per explicit source/configuration group,
selected by access order rather than outcomes. This is a convenience schema
sample, not a representative deployment cohort. Inspect at least two distinct
configurations if cheap pinned access permits it. Public cards already displayed
example outcomes to the researchers; disclose that incidental prior exposure.
No policy selection, fitting, cost-quality comparison or confirmation uses these
rows. Outcomes remain recorded verifier labels; no generated model results.

Require response revision identity to match a pinned dataset revision for an
accepted sample. Reject truncated cells for conclusions needing their contents.
Limit the new small-record access phase to 16 MiB of returned response bodies;
never fetch the entire Parquet corpus or expensive message-column chunks solely
to answer a schema question. Metadata/footer attempts are logged separately.

Distinguish billable usage, reconstructed text tokens, and step/tool proxies.
Missing per-call context/cache/reasoning usage must not be filled with current
prices or described as measured savings. Gate B fails if available proxies
cannot identify the proposed cost-risk mechanism without arbitrary assumptions.
Only after Gate B passes would the irreversible abandon/original-continuation
intervention, frozen development settings and pilot be implemented. The stated
95% retention target and approximately 5% cost-per-retained-success criterion
come from the brief and must not move after results are seen.

## S004 — STOP at Gate B for the predeclared expense question

The clean source-bound validation at `6e96f44` retrieved the same two complete
engineering-example records and pinned cards. Header revisions and original
payload hashes matched; the convenience rows have ordered tool interactions
and recorded outcomes, but no structured call usage, timing or cumulative cost.
Metadata/footer checks agree for the inspected representations. A matched,
linked two-configuration cohort remains unestablished; the available groups
must not be represented as an isolated model-change experiment.

Missing dollar billing alone is not fatal, and mixed workload shifts are allowed.
An interaction-count/token replay could answer a narrower descriptive question.
It cannot identify S002's heterogeneous remaining execution-expense mechanism:
repeated context, cache treatment, omitted reasoning, compaction and tool runtime
are not recoverable from these fields. The weaker replay has no established
distinguishing contribution beyond existing transcript-token economics and
selective-label auditing. We therefore stop rather than silently change the
estimand or invent a proxy finding. This is not evidence that stopping is
unprofitable, that all public data are inadequate, or that count-based research
is intrinsically invalid. The two example failures are not a success-rate estimate.

No policy fitting, threshold/audit-budget selection, cost-quality replay,
confirmation, manuscript, remote publication or outreach follows this decision.
The remaining work is evidence packaging and local reproducibility checks only.
