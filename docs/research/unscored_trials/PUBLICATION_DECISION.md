# Research gate: REPORT

Retain a technical report. Do not prepare or submit a TMLR manuscript from this
decision study. The narrower final-release audit is feasible and reproducible;
the original-attempt and retry questions remain data-blocked. This is a completed
research decision, not a claim that all desirable data have been recovered.

The strongest objection is that the closest audit already studies this release's
denominators and rank sensitivity, while the additional raw-record reconciliation
mostly identifies export, schema and identity differences whose causes cannot be
established. Native null-zero scoring reproduces on the strict cohort. Equal-task
and pooled policies give the same primary scores. The completion envelopes apply
established algebra to a hypothetical target; they do not reveal corrected ranks.
These results support a careful technical report, but do not yet demonstrate a
distinct, actionable empirical lesson sufficient to justify submission work.

The useful additional result is an auditable separation of numerical agreement
from population/identity agreement. The retained release permits raw final-record
checking that summary-only work cannot perform, but an unreconciled export is not
proof of an evaluation-policy error. Keep every exclusion and disagreement visible
and avoid promoting diagnostic whole-job pooling to native group reproduction.

The [findings](FINDINGS.md), [claim ledger](CLAIM_LEDGER.md),
[closest-work comparison](RELATED_WORK.md) and
[reproduction guide](REPRODUCING.md) are the deliverable. Preserve the original
baseline and its historical artifacts. Corrections to its interpretation are
documented here; it has not been silently rewritten.

Reopen the gate only with a specific evidence-bearing advance:

- For original accounting: an exact planned roster, durable attempt/retry lineage,
  submitted harness versions and a pinned importer/adjudication history relevant
  to the claimed target. A minimum repetition rule is insufficient.
- For a submission: demonstrate why the source-level reconciliation changes a
  substantive conclusion or resolves a recurring uncertainty beyond the closest
  audit. More benchmark rows alone do not meet that test.
- Before authorship/publication claims: human source-to-table verification and
  responsibility for the text; the current worksheet remains pending. This is
  separate from the software checks already completed.

No outreach, paid evaluation, paper submission or conditional manuscript stage
was undertaken. The decision stops this bounded study; it does not spend the
remaining allowance looking for a more favorable benchmark.
