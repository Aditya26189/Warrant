## Result and interpretation

The full census has 217 joint native matches, 20 unresolved jobs and eight
unavailable jobs. Joint mismatch includes populations, identity/indexes and
counters; it is not a numerical score-error count. Agreement on the strict
cohort is partly an inclusion condition. The separate post hoc whole-job result
remains 236 numeric matches, three differences and six unavailable; it neither
changes eligibility nor reconstructs the official website leaderboard.

All 56 retained submissions have uniform within-submission repetitions: 53 have
five records per task and three have one. Equal-task and pooled weights coincide
algebraically. This is an implementation check for the selected cohort, not
general evidence against weighting sensitivity. Of 1,540 candidate pairs, 109
have incompatible task-definition/checksum signatures; all 1,431 compatible
pairs are retained. Their classes remain 1,360 strict robust, 56
reversal-compatible, 14 equality-compatible and one identified tie. The 14 split
into eight strict orders that can become ties and six native ties that can
break. No corrected ranking was recovered.

The strongest objection remains closest-work overlap plus unknown export causes.
Issue 2246 and honestboard already examine denominator/ranking sensitivity in
this release. These extra reconciliation checks have not established a new
actionable benchmark defect or changed a substantive benchmark conclusion.
REPORT is the appropriate stopping point.
