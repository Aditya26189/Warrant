# Feasibility answer: STOP at Gate B

The question was whether acquiring censored feedback after executor/workload
change supports a substantial empirical contribution. A specific cost/risk
question survived literature review, but the inspected released representations
do not identify its remaining-execution-expense mechanism. Stop this study.
The [decision](../DECISION.md) states the qualifications and strongest objection.

We inspected supplied and additional primary literature, available monitor
artifacts, pinned dataset cards/trees, bounded Parquet footers and two complete
revision-bound records. The final collector/inspection used clean `6e96f44`;
body hashes matched the earlier engineering examples. The [schema table](../SCHEMA.md),
[source manifest](../results/source_manifest.json) and [resources](../results/resources.json)
carry the observations and their limits. Raw trajectories remain outside Git.

What changed our belief was the combination of strong overlap and insufficient
cost observability. The audit mathematics is substantially established, including
a direct finite-population mapping; threshold transfer and transcript-token
economics are also covered. The released records can support interaction-count
replay, but do not distinguish actual expense covariance after shift. We did
not infer that stopping is unprofitable or make a policy performance claim.

No classifier, stopping threshold, audit-budget grid, retention interval,
economics replay, confirmation cohort, scientific policy plot or manuscript was
produced. These conditional stages were not reached. The [protocol status](../PROTOCOL.md)
and [claim ledger](../CLAIM_EVIDENCE.md) prevent a feasibility inspection from
being presented as a preregistered or confirmatory result.

The strongest reviewer objection is that an explicit count-based estimand would
be valid. It would, but its substantive difference from the closest work was not
established; merely running it would not answer the narrowed question. Further
investment needs comparable step-level accounting, or an independently motivated
different question rather than a post-failure rewrite.

The new offline tests and Ruff passed. A provenance-path defect was caught by
the first real invocation before downloads, repaired and regression-tested;
NVIDIA v1.2 access failures and an invalid early memory measurement are retained.
No full repository test-suite claim is made. Incremental paid API/GPU spend was
INR 0; no experimental model inference or remote publishing occurred.
