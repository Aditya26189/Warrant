# Whole-work identity and reused research

25 October 2026. Interpretation before the pending shared-chapter identity
adjudication. No work is merged, cleared, selected or newly added by this note.

The approved frame deduplicates DOI/arXiv/forum crosslinks, then normalized title
plus author identity. Its unit is one research manuscript/work with one selected
version. It does not define a new unit of statistically independent experiments.
The extraction codebook separately records duplicate/cluster information.

Apply that distinction consistently:

* A primary citation or a chapter explicitly based on a paper establishes a
  source relationship. It does not by itself establish that the whole documents
  are versions of the same work. Compare their whole titles, authorship,
  document scope and primary version history before deduplicating.
* A broader thesis and a narrower paper can be distinct whole works with shared
  research. If primary evidence establishes that relationship, preserve both
  frozen work IDs/memberships and record the shared-study edge. Do not silently
  merge the entire thesis into one chapter or create extra works for chapters.
* The locked domain includes original empirical research manuscripts/preprints;
  it has no separate thesis exclusion. Any included document must still support
  the domain criteria in its selected edition. A source's explicit reuse note
  does not authorize transfer of the paper's method facts into the thesis, or
  vice versa. A paper version unspecified in a chapter note stays unspecified.
* Shared underlying research is not independent replication or new independent
  evidence. Preserve that limitation for later duplicate/cluster recording if
  either work is selected. The fixed paper-level feasibility gates are unchanged;
  do not substitute a study denominator, redraw related works, or make literature
  prevalence/independence claims.

An actual correction must bind the prior row, exact primary scope/version facts,
the type of reuse relationship and its limitations. A genuinely uncertain whole-
work identity remains UNCLEAR. The original intake/reviewer questions and the
earlier custodian hold remain immutable historical records. Complete-frame
approval is still required before any draw; no B2 work is authorized.
